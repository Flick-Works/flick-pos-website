Core Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Store

Designed from the ground up for Indian retail. Three powerful modules, one seamless experience.

Lightning Checkout

Process sales in under 50 milliseconds. Barcode scanning, quick-add items, and instant billing — no waiting, no freezing.

  • Barcode & search-based item entry
  • Instant bill generation
  • Multi-payment mode support

Real-time Inventory

Every sale automatically updates your stock. Know exactly what's in, what's low, and what's out — in real time.

  • Auto stock deduction on sale
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Bulk import & category management

Khata — Customer Ledger

Track credit, payments, and outstanding balances for every customer. Digital Khata that's always accurate and instantly accessible.

  • Per-customer credit tracking
  • Payment history & reminders
  • One-tap balance settlement
